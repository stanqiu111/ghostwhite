Federated social network · CMPUT 404

Your posts live on your server.
They still reach everyone else.

ÉTHÉRÉ is a node, not a site. It talks to other nodes over a plain HTTP inbox protocol, so following someone on another server works the same as following someone on this one.

How a post travels

No central server sits in the middle. Each node stores its own authors and pushes copies outward to the people who asked for them.

You publish

The entry is written to this node's database with a visibility you pick per post.

We resolve your followers

Followers on other nodes are looked up, and the entry's visibility decides which of them qualify.

It lands in their inbox

A copy is POSTed to /api/authors/<id>/inbox on each remote node.

They read it locally

Their node serves it from its own storage, so their feed keeps working if this one goes down.

What you can do here

The parts below are built and running on this node, not a roadmap.

Decide who sees each post

Visibility is set per entry, not once for the whole account, and it is enforced on the server for local and federated readers alike.

Public
anyone, including logged out
Unlisted
anyone with the link
Friends only
mutual follows

Stream your screen

Go live over WebRTC with a bullet-curtain comment track that scrolls across the video for every watcher at once.

Transport
WebRTC, REST signalling
Comment length
120 characters
Delivery
every 1.5 s

Write in Markdown or images

Entries render Markdown safely, and images are stored inline so they travel with the post to other nodes.

Text
plain, Markdown
Images
PNG, JPEG (base64)

Bring your GitHub activity

Connect a GitHub username and your public activity is imported as entries, so your feed reflects what you actually shipped.

Approve your own followers

Follows are requests you accept or decline. Accept in both directions and you are friends, which unlocks friends-only posts.

Keep your profile to yourself

Share your bio, avatar and follower lists with everyone, with accepted followers only, or with no one. Your name stays visible so your posts are still attributable.
